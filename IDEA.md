# NALAM AI — நலம் AI

## The problem
Rural and peri-urban communities in Tamil Nadu face three overlapping health barriers:
1. **Language**: health information is rarely available in Tamil at the right literacy level.
2. **Literacy**: medicine labels and prescriptions are written in technical English that patients cannot parse.
3. **Access**: the nearest doctor may be hours away; a phone is closer.

The result: people double doses, stop medicines early, ignore emergency symptoms, or pay for private care when free government services exist.

## What NALAM AI does
NALAM AI is a bilingual (Tamil + English) health information assistant that runs on any smartphone. It:
- Explains prescriptions in plain language (never inventing instructions)
- Identifies medical emergencies and routes the user to 108 (ambulance) with fixed, code-owned text — no model involved
- Refuses to diagnose or prescribe — explicitly and firmly
- Answers preventive health questions with curated, cited guidance
- Tells users where to get free medicines and government-funded care

## Who it is for
Tamil-speaking adults in Tamil Nadu with limited health literacy, especially:
- Patients managing chronic conditions (BP, diabetes, thyroid)
- Caregivers of elderly relatives
- Parents of young children
- People with limited access to healthcare facilities

## Measurable social good
- Emergency cases are handed off in < 10 ms (code path, no model latency)
- Every guidance item is cited and traceable to a public health authority source
- Free government facility information reduces unnecessary private spending
- Plain-language prescription explanation increases medicine adherence
